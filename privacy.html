Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Lord & Grace ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and phone number when you sign up or place an order.
  • Order information: items purchased, delivery address, and order history.
  • Payment information: payments are processed securely by Flutterwave. We do not store your card details on our servers.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To send you updates about your order status via email or in-app notification.
  • To respond to customer service inquiries.

How we protect your information

Your account and order data is stored securely using Firebase, a Google Cloud service, with access restricted by security rules. Payment processing is handled entirely by Flutterwave, a licensed payment service provider.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share necessary delivery details (name, phone, address) with delivery personnel to fulfill your order.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the details below.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, reach us via WhatsApp at the number listed on our homepage.
