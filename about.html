About Lord & Grace

All You Need, All In One Place.

Lord & Grace Multipurpose Enterprises started with a simple idea: shopping for everyday essentials shouldn't mean running from store to store across Ibadan. From our shop on Dick Road, Aleshinloye Way, we bring together cosmetics, toiletries, footwear, makeup, clothing, perfume, and phone accessories under one roof — and now, on one website.

We know the frustration of paying inflated prices for basic items, or discovering a product is out of stock after a long trip across town. That's exactly what we set out to fix: genuinely affordable prices, real stock availability you can check before you leave home, and a shopping experience that respects your time.

Our story

Lord & Grace began as a physical shop serving the Aleshinloye community, built on word-of-mouth trust and repeat customers who knew they'd find quality products at fair prices. As more customers asked whether they could order without visiting in person, we built this online store to bring that same reliability to anyone in Nigeria — with home delivery and secure online payment, while keeping the personal service that got us here.

What we stand for

Quality products

We personally select what we stock — nothing makes it onto our shelves or our site that we wouldn't use ourselves.

Fair pricing

No inflate-then-discount pricing games. What you see is a genuinely fair price.

Real customer service

Questions before or after your order go straight to a real person on WhatsApp — not an automated bot.

Reliable delivery

Every order includes real-time tracking and a delivery verification code, so you always know where your package is.
